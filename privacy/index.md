# Thaler Privacy Policy

_Effective date: October 29, 2026._


Thaler is a money notebook that works entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your data.

## What Thaler collects

Nothing. Thaler has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer
receives no information about you or about how you use the app.

## Where your data is kept

Everything you enter (accounts, transactions, budgets, categories, settings) is stored only on your device, in the
app's own storage. It is not sent to any server. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

## Backups

You can export a backup file from Settings → Backup & export. The file is created on your device and goes only where
you choose to save or share it (for example Files, iCloud Drive or another app). Keep it somewhere safe: anyone who
has the file can read your entries.

## Importing from another app

Importing history from another app is optional. If you use it, you give your old app's export file and Thaler's
prompt to an AI assistant of your choice, outside Thaler. That service's own privacy policy applies to what you send
it. Thaler does not send anything itself; it only reads the file you bring back.

## Children

Thaler is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
