# Thaler

A money notebook that stays on your phone. Log what you spend and earn, keep every currency apart, and see where the
month went. No sign-in, no bank links, everything stays on your device.

## Support

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? [Open an issue](https://github.com/yet300/thaler/issues/new) — please include
your iPhone model, iOS version and Thaler version (shown on its App Store page).

Your data lives only on your phone, so the developer can't see or restore it. To move to a new phone, export a backup
in Settings → Backup & export and restore it there.

## Privacy

Thaler collects nothing. Read the [privacy policy](privacy/).
